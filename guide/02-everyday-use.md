# Everyday use

## The desktop

The taskbar at the bottom holds **Start**, search, **Task View**, your pinned
and running programs, the tray icons and the clock. Right-click the taskbar
for its settings, or open **Settings > Personalization > Taskbar**.

- Click a program's button to bring its window forward, again to minimize it.
- **Shift+click** or a **middle click** on a running program's button opens
  another window of it.
- Right-click a button for its menu: pin or unpin it, **Report a problem**,
  close its windows.

**Start** lists every program, Windows or Linux. Type to search programs and
settings. Right-click a program to pin it to Start or the taskbar, run it as
an administrator, open its file location, or set it to **start at sign-in**.

## The look

**Settings > Personalization** chooses the look as a whole (**Themes**: Classic,
Rounded, Horizon, Glass) or each part on its own -- window frames, Start, the
taskbar. Mixing parts makes the theme "Custom".

**Background** offers a picture, a solid color, a picture lit through the
glass where the pointer is, or **Living glass**: the picture as stained glass
in the project's colours, each window outlined by its own leaded border.
Moving backgrounds stop on battery saver and under full-screen programs.

## Files

Your files are in your home folder, which is also your Windows profile:
Documents, Pictures, Downloads and the rest are the same folders whichever
kind of program you use. **File Explorer** (Win+E) shows them.

- Tabs: **Ctrl+T** opens one, **Ctrl+W** closes one, **Ctrl+Tab** moves
  between them. Each tab remembers its own back and forward.
- **Ctrl+N** opens another window.
- USB sticks and discs appear under **This PC** with a drive letter.

## When something hangs

- **Task Manager** (Ctrl+Shift+Esc) ends a program: select it and press
  **End task**. A program that does not close when asked -- Windows or Linux
  -- is ended after a few seconds.
- **Ctrl+Alt+Backspace** ends all of your Windows programs at once and brings
  the desktop back. Linux programs and other people's sessions are not
  touched. Use it when the desktop itself stops answering.
- **Ctrl+Alt+F2** gives a text console where you can sign in and look around,
  even when the desktop is frozen.

## Voice typing

**Win+H** types what you say into the program in front, offline -- nothing
leaves the computer. Set it up (and test the microphone) in **Control Panel >
Speech Recognition**.
